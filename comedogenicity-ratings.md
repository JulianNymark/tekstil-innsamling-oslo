## Important Caveats

1. **Concentration matters**: Comedogenicity is dose-dependent. An ingredient rated 2 at 100% may be 0 at 2.5% concentration.
2. **Formula synergy**: Pairs of ingredients (e.g., Steareth-2 + Steareth-20, or cetearyl alcohol + ceteareth-20) can be more comedogenic together than individually.
3. **Individual variation**: Acne-prone skin may react to ingredients others tolerate.
4. **Fulton scale limitations**: Most published comedogenicity data comes from rabbit ear assays from the 1970s-80s, which may not perfectly translate to human skin.
5. **"Not rated" does not mean "zero"**: Some ingredients lack published data simply because they haven't been tested on the Fulton scale.
